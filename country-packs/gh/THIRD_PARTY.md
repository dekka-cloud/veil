# Third-party data in the Ghana pack

## libphonenumber

`numbering.toml` contains the Ghana (GH) mobile and fixed-line number patterns from
Google's libphonenumber metadata, release v9.0.42 (27 October 2026),
<https://github.com/google/libphonenumber>.

## GhanaPost GPS district codes

`digital-address.toml` lists the region and district codes GhanaPostGPS publishes at
<https://www.ghanapostgps.com/regions-and-district-codes/> (retrieved 4 November 2026).
They are factual codes used to validate the shape of a digital address. Veil doesn't
resolve addresses or contact GhanaPostGPS.
